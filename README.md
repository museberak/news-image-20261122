A broadsheet front page, "THE WORLD", with 35 verified stories from every
continent: COP31 collapses with no deal in Antalya, Iran hits Bahrain, a
Russian drone hits Kyiv flats, Verstappen wins a breathless Las Vegas Grand
Prix. A real world map with 35 numbered pins (Europe and the U.S. East Coast
enlarged in insets), a COP31-by-the-numbers infographic, and a diverging
markets chart. Every fact was checked against Reuters, AP, BBC, CNN, UN News
and other primary sources; no AI image generator was used for the page or its
text. `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the shipped
`entry.png` byte for byte to `/output/entry.png` and checks the copy's size,
leading bytes and full contents before finishing.
